Principles

The test for every screen: would a 60-year-old visiting professor use this on day 1 without being told how?

  • One glance, one tap

    Where to be now sits at the top. Getting there is one large button.

  • Never colour alone

    Now, delayed, room changed, cancelled and ended each have a word and an icon.

  • The door, not the building

    The entrance photo and indoor steps come before Google Maps.

  • IST is the truth

    Times are computed and shown in India time, whatever the phone's timezone is set to.

  • Brand on the frame

    The gradient frames the screen. Content sits on white or deep navy, where it's easy to read.

Colour

Restrained, with one committed band. Navy carries the interface, purple means "now", and the deep gradient frames the top of the screen. Contrast ratios are computed live on this page using WCAG 2.1.

Gradients

Deep gradient: UI#141d62 → #520273 · 120°From the dangler. Header band, closing state, icon background.
Bright gradient: icon and splash only#013f96 → #77019bFrom the unipole and backdrop. Never behind text in the app.

Icons and brand motifs

Lucide icons with a 2 px stroke and rounded caps, in currentColor. Two motifs come from the print work. Both are decorative and hidden from screen readers.

Motifs

Four-point sparkle (dangler). Header band and closing state only.
Dot-wave (posters). At most 12% opacity, inline SVG.

Motion

Motion only explains a change of state. It uses the ease-out curve cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1), exits are faster than entrances, and nothing loops. With reduced motion turned on, nothing moves; things simply fade.
